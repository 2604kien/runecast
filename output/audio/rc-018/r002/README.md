Choosing another player or pausing a sequence player cancels the sequence. Individual native volume controls remain available; moving the shared slider reapplies its value to all players.

These are raw original provider MP3 outputs. Source levels are not matched, no catalogue mixer gains or normalization are applied, and no lossless-original or 24-bit-fidelity claim is made. The r002 source files remain unchanged. Owner approval covers this direction; full-library listening, final mix balance and gameplay integration are separate.
