About this audition

These five text-generated candidates explore a new sound direction. They are unchanged provider MP3s, with no claim of a lossless original or 24-bit fidelity. The comparison WAVs are frozen copies of the delivered r001 procedural assets, preserved independently of active runtime replacements. The current in-game Cast tone is a separate placeholder.

Only one player runs at a time. The candidate sequence leaves 0.8 seconds between sounds. Using a different player or pausing the sequence cancels it; Stop all resets every player. Shared review volume starts at 50%. Native volume controls can adjust an individual player until the shared slider is moved again.

Provider durations shown here may differ slightly from browser-reported MP3 duration because of encoding padding. The owner approved this five-cue direction; that approval does not establish acceptance of the complete replacement library or final mix. The raw candidates here preserve their original timing and levels. This page adds no gameplay bindings.
