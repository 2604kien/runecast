## Remaining differences and limits

The r006 reconstruction follows the supplied composition and shared material direction; it is not pixel-identical to the supplied reference. It retains r004 crypt/material sources and the r002 actor, regular repeated tile bevels, cleaner vector icon details, geometric sun rays and Noto Serif letterforms. The title uses mixed case instead of the reference's exact small-cap appearance. Painted highlights, edge wear, actor detail and some spacing therefore differ. These are visible matching-style/component-construction differences, not hidden omissions of the new clean-board request.

The native 360 px pair is an explicit scrolling layout study. It compresses the enemy header and does not display the main's separate BOSS line; only one detailed hand card is visible with an external hand-scroll caption. It is not a claim that the entire 841 px composition fits at once in 360×640. Input, scrolling behavior, actual device font rendering and runtime connection rules were not tested in this review.

Home, Map and Menu were not rebuilt in r006. Their navy/cream/gold, turquoise-primary and serif material relationships remain compatible with the reused r004 sources; their exact scene placements and dimmed-menu interaction remain outside this PNG review. This review does not approve the reconstruction on the owner's behalf, supersede the supplied artwork, or claim production/runtime completion. Only this r006 review document was edited by the independent reviewer; no Git operations or runtime edits were performed.
