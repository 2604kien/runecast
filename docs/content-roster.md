“Permanent” means an owned card in the **current run**, as opposed to a generated temporary rune. All 12 definitions are available to the run's content pools from the start; there are no profile unlocks or across-run card inventories. A new run owns eight cards: two each of Spark, Shield, Focus and Conjure Spark. Copies have separate instance identities for upgrades/removal. Definition IDs never stand in for those instance identities.

The proposed route visits nine rooms: four normal encounters, one normal/elite branch choice, one event, one shop, one recovery room and one guardian. Alternate route nodes do not add visited rooms. This stays inside the 8–10-room planning budget. No treasure room is added: a chest in a visual reference can label a reward/treasure symbol without becoming another content family.

**H31 provisional connector kits:** each playable turn independently selects one equally likely kit with Straight/Corner/Split/Join counts **6/2/1/1**, **4/4/1/1** or **4/2/2/2**. Each contains ten physical pieces and at least one matched Split/Join pair. The normal rune-card hand is drawn separately. Placement uses the current kit; erasing/replacing a connector returns it to supply and Undo restores counts. Rotation, erasing and Undo stay free. Unused connectors do not bank across turns; the next playable turn receives a fresh kit. Connector supply does not enter permanent card ownership or draw/discard piles. RC-007 implements these owner-accepted provisional values in place of unlimited production straight/corner supply; they add no rune definition, board family, relic or currency. Equal counts do not prove equal usefulness, and geometric feasibility does not establish damaging hands or balanced play. See the [starter-resource contract](v1-design-spec.md#tutorial-collection-and-initial-resources).

An authored example is one reproducible construction witness with explicit seed, hand, kit, energy and commands. The [board catalog](board-catalog.md) records the executable examples, launches, expected results and validation limits.

## Economy and service contract

Gold is the only currency, starts at 0, belongs to the current run and is discarded when that run ends. Display exact price, balance, eligible card instance and resulting value before committing. No purchase can go below zero Gold. Shop stock is three distinct rune definitions and one unowned relic, determined on room entry and saved; each slot can be bought once. Removal and upgrade can each be used once per shop visit. Exhausted pools leave a clearly unavailable slot; leaving remains possible. There is no refresh button or persistent/global store. Five normal victories yield 75 Gold before the shop, or four normals plus elite yield 90, before event effects. Buying the early event relic for 15 leaves at least 60 at the shop. This makes every individual service reachable, while choosing combinations remains meaningful; RC-036/RC-045 must test the resulting choices and difficulty.
