# RC-017 actor, attachment and scenery contract

October 16, 2026. Production Combat uses the original approved-atlas neutral and the owner-retained RC-014 attack/hit paintings. No character PNG, source painting or generated environment PNG changed. The superseded generated neutral stays retired. The hit painting remains damage feedback; it does not implement a dodge outcome.

## Calling the actor

Instantiate [character.tscn](../assets/enemies/shadeling/character.tscn), set `reference_neutral = true` before adding it to the arena, and wait for `_ready`. The root is the fixed foot. The arena owns its position, uniform scale and visibility; effects operate on `PoseRoot`. No method receives a model or writes gameplay. Sequencing, event identities, reduced-motion preference, cancellation and completion belong to the production presenter.

| API | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `sample_presentation(state, progress, reduced=false)` | Deterministic normalized sample of `idle`, `attack`, `hit` or `defeat`; stops local playback; no signals, timers or callbacks. Invalid states and nonfinite values are rejected. |
| `reset_presentation(battle_state="playing", reduced=false)` | Restore texture, region, mask, placement, sprite flips, transforms, tint and visibility. Playing starts neutral idle; player victory leaves the defeated enemy faded at its terminal sample; player defeat keeps the surviving enemy stationary. |
| `set_idle_enabled(enabled, reduced=false)` | Enables only the nonblocking idle loop, or stops it on its current frame. Reduced idle is stationary and disables `_process`. |
| `get_attachment_position(name, global=false)` | Current pose attachment in actor-root-local coordinates, or global canvas coordinates when requested. |
| `show_pose`, `sample_state`, `play_state`, `stop_preview` | Retained RC-014 isolated-study API. Study durations are historical and do not set production action timing. |

Call `sample_presentation("hit", …)` only for an actual positive `damage.applied`. Requested damage and zero applied damage are separate presenter feedback cases. Attack anticipation/contact/recovery follows the real retaliation event. No pose completion unlocks input. Hit remains on its contact pose until the presenter explicitly restores the actor; defeat stays faded until an explicit new encounter/reset. `_exit_tree()` stops idle and drops optional warmed-pose references.

[motion.tres](../assets/enemies/shadeling/motion.tres) exposes editable Godot `Curve` resources and study timings through [motion.gd](../assets/enemies/shadeling/motion.gd): idle 2.0 s, attack 0.32 s, hit 0.16 s and defeat 0.45 s. Production samples those curves with normalized progress driven by [combat_feedback_style.gd](../scenes/vfx/combat_feedback_style.gd): attack **1.50 s**, hit **1.50 s** and terminal 0.75 s after the October 17 owner pacing follow-up. Attack contact occupies normalized 0.28–0.72, giving 0.42 s anticipation, 0.66 s contact pose and 0.42 s recovery. Geometric anticipation is at most 0.012 radians and contact squash 1.2%. Hit uses a local warm tint with at most 0.016 radians. Idle breathes 0.4% about the foot, approximately 1.27 design pixels at the neutral head. Hit/attack return to neutral before the next event; idle stays stopped until the action completes. Reduced motion keeps the approved neutral silhouette throughout attack/hit/terminal samples and uses only local colour/opacity feedback; all rotation, stretch, travel and bobbing are removed. Audio is independent.

World feedback attaches to these markers; player feedback attaches to live stat controls. There is no visible player character, collision point or damage authority in these coordinates.

All poses share the current arena mapping: full composition canvas at `(254,92)` with scale `0.75`; crop `Rect(0,60,841,450)` gives crop-local foot `(446,368)`. Resize by a single `W/841` factor. ## Verification and costs

Invoke through `tests/actor_animation_runner.gd`; [results](../output/qa/rc-017/actor/results.json). `tests/actor_animation_capture.gd` renders supplemental arena compositing and proxy evidence. These supplement the actual playable Combat motion captures; they do not replace that integration proof.

The updated full `tests/ui_art_integration_capture.gd` regression passed **2,596 checks with zero failures** at 720×1600, 450×1000 and 360×640: [results](../output/qa/rc-017/art-regression/results.json). Its current contract verifies nonblocking bounded idle, warmed attack/hit textures, correct terminal pose state and actual rendered compositing. It confirms **214,546 original outer-scenery pixels unchanged** outside the documented feather/UI exclusions and **101,088 clean-center pixels** with zero RGB error against the empty plate. Historical claims of source identity everywhere outside the actor silhouette are superseded. Representative opening, victory and defeat stills at these sizes were visually reviewed; the preserved narrow layouts use the existing scrolling behavior. Its observed consumer texture estimate including warmed attack/hit textures is 21,666,888 decoded RGBA8 bytes, excluding font/engine/render-target overhead and unrelated unused assets.
